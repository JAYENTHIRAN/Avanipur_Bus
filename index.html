About Avanipur Transit

About Avanipur Bus Details

This website provides a simple timetable reference for bus services connecting Avanipur with Tindivanam and Chennai.

Avanipur is connected via regular town and loop buses (13D, 6B, 23) operating towards the major transit hub of Tindivanam, and a dedicated once-daily direct connection to Chennai (Bus 189).

Important Notice: Timings are based on the timetable entered into this application. Please verify timings locally because bus schedules may change due to traffic conditions, local festival schedules, or transport department revisions.
